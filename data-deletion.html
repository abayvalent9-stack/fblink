Data Deletion — Adsup

Adsup is a private application used by its owner to manage the owner's own Facebook Pages and advertising accounts. It does not collect or store personal data about other Facebook users.

How to request deletion

If you believe any data relating to you has been processed by Adsup and you want it deleted:

  1. Send an email to abayvalent9@gmail.com with the subject "Adsup Data Deletion Request".
  2. Include your Facebook profile URL or the Page/post concerned so we can locate any related data.
  3. We will confirm receipt, delete any data found, and confirm completion by email within 30 days.

Privacy Policy — Adsup

Effective date: 9 November 2026

What Adsup is

Adsup is a private application operated by its owner to manage the owner's own Facebook Pages and advertising accounts through Meta's official APIs. It is used to publish the owner's own content (such as videos and posts) to the owner's Pages and to read advertising performance reports for the owner's ad accounts.

Data the app accesses

The app accesses, through Meta's APIs and only for the owner's own assets:

  • Content the owner publishes to the owner's Facebook Pages (posts, videos, and their publishing status).
  • Advertising performance metrics for the owner's ad accounts (spend, impressions, clicks, and campaign structure).
  • Aggregate Page and post insights provided by Meta.

The app does not collect personal data from members of the public, does not track individuals, and does not build profiles of Facebook users.

How data is used

Data accessed through the app is used solely to operate and report on the owner's own content and advertising. Data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and is never used to profile or target individuals outside the owner's own advertising accounts on Meta's platform.

Storage and retention

Performance data is stored on the owner's own computer systems and retained only as long as needed for the owner's business reporting. Access is restricted to the owner.

Your choices and data deletion

Because the app processes only the owner's own Page and advertising data, it does not hold personal data about other Facebook users. If you believe the app has processed data relating to you and you would like it deleted, contact us at the email below and we will respond and delete any such data within 30 days. See also our Data Deletion page.
